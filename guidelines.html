Author Guidelines

  1. Manuscripts must be original and have not been published elsewhere.
  2. Articles may be written in Indonesian or English.
  3. The manuscript length is 5,000–8,000 words.
  4. The manuscript should contain:
    • Title
    • Abstract
    • Keywords
    • Introduction
    • Research Method
    • Results and Discussion
    • Conclusion
    • References
  5. References must use APA 7th Edition style.
  6. Authors are encouraged to use reference managers such as Mendeley or Zotero.
  7. Manuscripts should be submitted through the journal website.
