MENAWAN, JOURNAL OF ECONOMICS AND BUSINESS Journal of Research and Publication in Economics with e-ISSN: 3025-4728 and p-ISSN: 3025-5899 is a journal intended for the publication of scientific articles published by the Indonesian Association of Economic and Accounting Research. This journal is published twice a year (August and December).

MENAWAN, JOURNAL OF ECONOMICS AND BUSINESS is intended to disseminate, develop, and facilitate research findings in the fields of Economics and Accounting, serving as a medium for lecturers, teachers, researchers, and practitioners in the fields of Economics and Management from all over Indonesia to exchange information about the latest research results that have been conducted.
